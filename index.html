LANTERN // 17

A signal for people who notice what others skip.

Do not search for the loudest clue.

Rain arrived before the streetlights.

Every window kept a different hour.

After midnight, the station clock stopped.

Down below, someone had chalked a circle.

Between the cracks, a second message waited.

Look at the edges before the center.

Even silence can have instructions.
